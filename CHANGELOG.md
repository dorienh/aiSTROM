# Changelog

## 2.0 — October 2026

Rebuilt to follow the original paper closely. New Goals sheet for listing ideas and shortlisting the top n projects; one sheet per roadmap step with a column per shortlisted project; a final Decide sheet with risk-benefit plot, SWOT and portfolio decision. Prompts use the paper's terminology; post-2021 additions are marked as 2026 notes. New expense-claims example comparing three projects. 10 pages instead of 15. The instructor guide still describes version 1.0 and will be updated separately.

## 1.0 — September 2026

First organised public release: general-AI overview, fourteen fillable detail pages across seven dimensions, complete expense-claims example and instructor kit. Covers predictive, generative and agentic projects. Includes explicit provider-training/privacy prompts. Older 0.x files are not the current release.
